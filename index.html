Directions & Reservations

Find Us Along Samfya Road, Mansa

Easy to spot along the main Samfya Road (M3) corridor in Mansa, Luapula Province — watch for our green-and-white REEN LODGE signboard and red-brick colonnade.

Traveller Landmark Guide

  • From Mansa Town Centre: Follow Samfya Road (M3) east toward Samfya; look for our peach perimeter wall, REEN LODGE signboard, and red-brick storefront colonnade.
  • From Samfya / Lake Bangweulu: Continue straight into Mansa on the M3 — Reen Business Centre sits directly along Samfya Road before town-centre congestion.
  • From Chembe / Pedicle Road: Enter Mansa and connect onto Samfya Road (M3 East) for gated overnight parking, hot meals, and travel supplies.
